Our story

Street food with a designer finish

Thuba Rush Kota Joint is a kota spot on Pretorius Street. The food is stacked high, sauced generously and packed to travel, from classic boxes to towers that need two hands.

Every order is made fresh, and the kitchen runs Monday to Saturday from 8am to 8pm.
